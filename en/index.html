About this game

Tide House is a 3D dorm life game. Eight characters live in their own rooms. Chat with them, touch, give gifts, take photos, and invite them to the beach and the café. Raise your intimacy to unlock new conversations, stories, furniture and outfits.

Show the full description

Life in the room

  • Walk around freely and use sofas, beds and a kotatsu: 81 furniture interactions
  • She greets you when you come in; tap "Talk" for a daily conversation, with choices that branch
  • During an interaction, tap her head, hands and more for reactions and lines
  • Over 1,100 story scenes with Japanese voices

Intimacy and memories

  • Talking, touching, gifts, collecting and mini-games all raise intimacy (Lv1–15 per character)
  • Level up to unlock new conversations, stories, furniture and outfits
  • Special gifts unlock special stories; replay any memory, staged with its own cameras in her room
  • 245 collectibles hidden in the rooms, some only at night
  • Just here for the stories? Unlock everything in one tap, and tune the numbers yourself

Photo mode

  • Hundreds of poses and expressions, 6 filters, depth of field and freeze frame
  • Photos go to the in-app album; invited companions can join group shots

Going out together

  • Invite others to the same place: they sit on sofas and beds or stand nearby watching you, and chat with each other
  • Volleyball and a water slide at the beach, dance photos at the café, a timed car wash
  • Room mini-games too: hand slap, rock-paper-scissors and scheduling

Phone

  • Chat with her, read and reply to her posts, and make video calls

Day and night

  • Day and night follow your phone's clock: lights come on and the windows go dark at night, or pin the time yourself

Outfits, places and furniture

  • 21 outfits and 14 places (8 rooms, the dorm hallway, a bathroom, the beach, a café and a car wash)
  • Any character can go anywhere; swap the beds, tables and sofas in each room

Also

  • Interface and lines in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, English and Japanese
  • No account, no ads, no in-app purchases; progress stays on your phone

Note: the app includes one character and her room. Pick other characters, outfits and places in "Downloads" (about 2.7 GB in total); Wi-Fi is recommended.

FAQ

Why does my computer show a security warning?

The desktop versions are not signed with a paid code-signing certificate, so Windows SmartScreen and macOS warn you once; allow it as described above. Each file has a SHA-256 checksum you can compare (Windows: certutil -hashfile FILE SHA256; Mac: shasum -a 256 FILE).

Why do I need to download more after installing?

To keep the app small, it includes one character and her room. Pick other characters, outfits and places in "Downloads" on the home screen; they are ready as soon as they finish. You can delete them too.

How do I update?

When a new version is out, "New version" appears under the title on the home screen and opens this page. Install the new version over the old one; saves and downloads are kept.

Where are my saves?

Windows: %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\潮聲小築; Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/Godot/app_userdata/潮聲小築; Android: the app's own storage (removed when you uninstall).
